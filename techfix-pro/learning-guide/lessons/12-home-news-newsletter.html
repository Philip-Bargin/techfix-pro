Lesson 12 · Building the Pages

Home Page, Part 3: News & Newsletter

Two more sections: a row of news cards with photos that zoom on hover, and a bright blue newsletter banner with a sign-up form.

  • About 50 minutes
  • Edits: index.html

By the end of this lesson you will be able to

  • Build image cards with <article>, overflow: hidden and a zoom-on-hover effect.
  • Place a label on top of a photo with absolute positioning.
  • Create soft decorative circles with radial-gradient().
  • Build a simple form with an email field and a button.

Style the news cards

Add to the <style> block:

  • Zoom effect: .news-image has a fixed height: 200px and overflow: hidden. On hover, the image inside is scaled to 108% (scale(1.08)). It grows, but the box stays the same size, so the extra is clipped and it looks like the camera zooms in.
  • Label on the photo: .news-image is position: relative, and .news-category is position: absolute; top: 1rem; left: 1rem;, which pins it to the photo's top-left corner.
  • .news-card:hover h3 { color: var(--primary); } turns the title blue when the card is hovered.
  • The “Read More” arrow: on hover, the gap between the text and the arrow grows from 0.4rem to 0.7rem, so the arrow slides right. Only gap is animated (transition: gap 0.3s ease).

Section 4 — Newsletter

Add the newsletter HTML

Directly above <!-- Footer -->, add:

Your first form

  • <form> wraps the input and button. action="#" means “stay on this page when submitted”. A real newsletter would send the form to a server, which needs more than HTML and CSS.
  • <input type="email"> is a text box that only accepts email addresses. Phones even show an @ key.
  • placeholder="…" is grey hint text that disappears when you type.
  • required means the form can't be sent while the box is empty.
  • <button type="submit"> sends the form.

Try it: click Subscribe with the box empty, or type hello (not a valid email). The browser shows a warning message all by itself, with no JavaScript needed!

Different from the original

The original site shows a JavaScript “Thanks for subscribing!” pop-up. Without JavaScript, a valid submission just reloads the page with # added to the address. That's expected.

Style the newsletter

Add to the <style> block:

  • The background is a diagonal linear-gradient from brand blue to dark blue.
  • Decorative circles: ::before and ::after each make a big circle (border-radius: 50%) filled with a radial-gradient, a faint white glow fading to transparent. They are placed partly outside the section (top: -50%, right: -10%), and overflow: hidden on the section crops them.
  • .newsletter-content gets position: relative; z-index: 1; so the text sits above the circles.
  • .newsletter-form is a flex row. flex: 1 on the input means “take up all the leftover space”, so the box stretches and the button keeps its natural width.
  • outline: none removes the default focus outline. (The Contact form in Lesson 18 adds a nicer focus style instead.)

Checkpoint

Common mistakes

  • The zoomed photo spills out of the card: .news-image needs overflow: hidden.
  • Photos are squashed or stretched: check object-fit: cover on .news-image img.
  • Newsletter text is hidden under the circles: .newsletter-content needs position: relative and z-index: 1.
