FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions. Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out directly.

How long does a typical repair take?

Most common repairs like screen replacements and battery swaps are completed within 30–60 minutes. More complex issues such as motherboard repairs or water damage recovery may take 1–3 business days. We'll give you an accurate timeline during your free diagnostic.

Do you offer a warranty on repairs?

Yes! All our repairs come with a 90-day warranty covering both parts and labor. If the same issue reoccurs within that period, we'll fix it at no additional cost. Business support plans include an extended 1-year warranty.

What brands do you repair?

We repair all major brands including Apple (iPhone, iPad, MacBook), Samsung, Google Pixel, OnePlus, Huawei, Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS, and Microsoft Surface. If you don't see your brand listed, contact us — we likely still service it.

Is the diagnostic really free?

Absolutely. We offer a completely free diagnostic assessment for all devices. You'll know exactly what's wrong and what it will cost to fix before any work begins. No obligation, no pressure.

Do I need to book an appointment?

Walk-ins are always welcome! However, booking an appointment guarantees priority service and reduces your wait time. You can book online through our contact form or give us a call.

Will my data be safe during repair?

Data security is our top priority. Our technicians follow strict privacy protocols, and we never access your personal files without explicit permission. For extra peace of mind, we recommend backing up your data before bringing your device in.
