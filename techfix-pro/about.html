Our Story

From a Small Workshop to Industry Leaders

TechFix Pro began in 2015 as a one-person repair shop in a garage. Founder Alex Chen believed that quality electronic repairs shouldn't cost a fortune. With a single toolkit and unwavering dedication, he started fixing devices for friends and neighbors.

Today, we've grown into a team of 25+ certified technicians across four locations, handling over 15,000 repairs annually. Despite our growth, our mission remains the same: deliver honest, high-quality repairs with transparent pricing and genuine parts.

We invest heavily in ongoing training for our technicians, ensuring they stay ahead of the curve with the latest devices and repair techniques. Every repair is backed by our 90-day warranty because we stand behind our work.
